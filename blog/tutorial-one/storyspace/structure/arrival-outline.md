## Objectives (skills, stated first)

1. How to audit a dependency chain for a relocated cost: the
   "steamless" clients drop Steam the launcher and keep Steam the
   purchase — their own READMEs say so.
2. How to sweep an ecosystem and know when it is empty: name the bars
   up front (renders rooms, mouse interaction, no proprietary assets,
   alive, speaks the private-server protocol), walk every candidate,
   record which bar each one fails.
3. How to recognize the moment a requirement forces a build: the one
   open, complete foundation (the official renderer, open-sourced with
   its images) had nothing in the ecosystem consuming it.

## The joke (his concept, for the opening; his pen finishes it)

I'm lazy: always use prior art. Prior art exists — none of it meets
spec (no purchase, mouse, works in the VM, alive). So I have to do it
myself. Doing it myself in 2026 means using prior art in a workable
way via klanker: we cleanroomed prior art — took the open pieces (the
official renderer and its images, the community's protocol knowledge)
and rebuilt the assembled thing to spec. The laziness never left; it
moved down a layer. The more things change…

Second edge, same story, verified in the record: laziness that
verifies is called reuse; laziness that doesn't is the borrowed LLM
summary that promised a CDN download and dissolved against the
READMEs.

## Arc (curated from the session, trimming only)

1. The requirement, in his words: remove the requirement to purchase
   Screeps; it must all work in the VM or it doesn't work.
2. The borrowed answer that didn't survive checking: an LLM's summary
   claimed a CDN download path; the actual READMEs refuse it
   ("you must own Screeps"). Evidence beat summary.
3. The sweep: every browser client traces to one 2019 codebase with
   mouse interaction unimplemented; the one complete client is a
   host-side unlicensed Unity app — rejected to keep "host needs only
   QEMU."
4. The turn: Screeps open-sourced their renderer and images,
   explicitly blessed for third-party clients — and the sweep found
   nothing that consumes it. The gap is the assignment.
